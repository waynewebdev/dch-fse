We Build Custom Homes That Become Family Legacies

Dynamic Custom Homes is a family-owned custom home builder in Central Texas. Owner Joshua Maas grew up in the San Antonio home building industry and is a third-generation builder, taught by his grandfather. In nearly 20 years building custom homes across San Antonio, Boerne, New Braunfels, and the Hill Country, we’ve stuck to one rule: build every home the way we’d build our own.

We keep each build personal, and our clients tend to become family. From the first design meeting to the final walkthrough, you’ll always know where your project stands and get straight answers on timelines and budget.

What We Do

Our Custom Home Building Services

Whether you’re planning a new custom home or a full renovation, Dynamic Custom Homes handles the entire build. We’re based in San Antonio and build across Central Texas, and we stay straight with you about budgets and timelines from day one.

Custom Home Building

Already have a plan? We build your custom home to spec on your lot anywhere in Central Texas — tailored to your vision, budget, and timeline.

Home Remodeling

Not your ordinary remodel — large-scale renovations and additions built to full custom-home standards for established homes.

Design-Build

No plans yet? We develop your design together from the start, then build it — one team, one contract, start to finish.

Luxury Homes

Thoughtfully elevated homes for move-up families — premium finishes and craftsmanship, typically in the $500K–$1M range.

Semi-Custom Homes

Personalize one of our proven floor plans — the flexibility of custom with predictable pricing and a faster timeline.

Renovation & Additions

Whole-home renovations, second-story additions, and major expansions — new-build discipline applied to your existing home.

Outdoor Living

Outdoor kitchens, covered patios, casitas, ADUs, and detached shops — built to the same standard as the main house.

Lot Consulting

Thinking about a lot? We evaluate slope, soil, utilities, and restrictions before you buy — so nothing surprises you later.
